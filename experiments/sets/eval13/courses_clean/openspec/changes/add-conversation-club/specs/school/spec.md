## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Conversation club

The system SHALL take sign-ups for the conversation club.

#### Scenario: Places left

- **WHEN** a student signs up for the conversation club
- **AND** places are left
- **THEN** the system adds the student to the club list

#### Scenario: Club full

- **WHEN** a student signs up for the conversation club
- **AND** no places are left
- **THEN** the system suggests the next club date
