# Requirements: Tickets

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a concert fan, I want to buy tickets online and enter the venue with my phone, so that I do not need paper tickets.

### Acceptance Criteria

- T1: WHEN a fan buys a ticket AND seats are left, THE Ticket System SHALL issue an e-ticket.
- T2: WHEN a fan buys a ticket AND no seats are left, THE Ticket System SHALL put the fan on the waiting list.
- T3: WHEN a fan scans an e-ticket at the entrance AND the e-ticket is valid, THE Ticket System SHALL open the gate.
- T4: WHEN a fan returns a ticket, THE Ticket System SHALL refund the price and offer the seat to the waiting list.
- T5: WHEN an e-ticket is scanned for the third time in a minute, THE Ticket System SHALL alert security.
