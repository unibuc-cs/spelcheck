# Requirements: Baggage

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a passenger, I want to drop my bag at a machine, so that I do not have to queue at the check-in desk.

### Acceptance Criteria

- B1: WHEN a passenger drops a bag at the machine AND the bag is within the weight limit, THE Baggage System SHALL accept the bag and print a bag tag.
- B2: WHEN a passenger scans a boarding pass AND the flight closes in less than 40 minutes, THE Baggage System SHALL send the passenger to the service desk.
- B3: WHEN the belt behind the machine jams for the third time in an hour, THE Baggage System SHALL call a technician.
- B4: WHEN a passenger presses the help button, THE Baggage System SHALL call an agent to the machine.
