# Requirements: Inspection

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a car owner, I want the yearly safety test of my car recorded online, so that I can prove that the car is roadworthy.

### Acceptance Criteria

- I1: WHEN an inspector finishes a test AND the car passes the test, THE Inspection System SHALL issue a certificate and set the date of the next test.
- I2: WHEN an inspector finishes a test AND the car fails the test, THE Inspection System SHALL list the defects and book a retest.
- I3: WHEN a certificate expires, THE Inspection System SHALL send the owner a reminder.
- I4: WHEN a car fails the test for the third time in a row, THE Inspection System SHALL report the car to the road authority.
- I5: WHEN an owner pays the test fee, THE Inspection System SHALL send a receipt by email.
- I6: WHEN an inspector finishes a test, THE Inspection System SHALL NOT issue a certificate.
