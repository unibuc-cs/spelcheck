## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Two vehicles

A request for two vehicles SHALL board those that may go.

#### Scenario: Example: two vehicles, one flagged

- **WHEN** lena asks to board vehicles v4 and v9 while v9 is flagged by customs at the start of the run
- **THEN** v4 is boarded
- **AND** v9 is not boarded
- **AND** the answer names v9 as not boarded
