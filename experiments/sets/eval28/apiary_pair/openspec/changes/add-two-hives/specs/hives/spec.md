## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Two hives

A request for two hives SHALL move those that may go.

#### Scenario: Example: two hives, one in quarantine

- **WHEN** tom asks to move hives h5 and h8 while h8 is in quarantine at the start of the run
- **THEN** h5 is moved
- **AND** h8 is not moved
- **AND** the answer names h8 as not moved
