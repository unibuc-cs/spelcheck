## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Two exhibits

A request for two exhibits SHALL lend those that may go.

#### Scenario: Example: two exhibits, one under restoration

- **WHEN** omar asks to lend exhibits x12 and x30 while x30 is under restoration at the start of the run
- **THEN** x12 is lent
- **AND** x30 is not lent
- **AND** the answer names x30 as not lent
