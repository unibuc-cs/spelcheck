# Requirements: Cards

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a cardholder, I want my bank card to work in shops and to be protected when something goes wrong, so that my money is safe.

### Acceptance Criteria

- C1: WHEN a cardholder pays with the card AND the card is not blocked AND the account has enough funds, THE Card System SHALL approve the payment.
- C2: WHEN a cardholder pays with the card AND the account does not have enough funds, THE Card System SHALL decline the payment.
- C3: WHEN a PIN check fails for the third time in a row, THE Card System SHALL block the card.
- C4: WHEN the cardholder unblocks the card in the banking app, THE Card System SHALL unblock the card.
- C5: WHEN a payment is made abroad, THE Card System SHALL send a notification to the cardholder.
- C6: WHEN a cardholder pays with the card AND the account has enough funds, THE Card System SHALL decline the payment.
