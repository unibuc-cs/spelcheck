# Requirements: Oven

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a cook, I want the oven to heat safely and to be controlled from my phone, so that I can cook without worry.

### Acceptance Criteria

- O1: WHEN the cook starts a program AND the oven door is closed and the child lock is off or the program was started from the phone app, THE Oven Controller SHALL heat the oven to the program temperature.
- O2: WHEN the oven door is opened during a program, THE Oven Controller SHALL pause the heating.
- O3: WHEN the temperature sensor fails to respond for the third time in a row, THE Oven Controller SHALL show an error and notify the cook.
- O4: WHEN the cook presses boost in the phone app, THE Oven Controller SHALL switch the heating on at full power.
- O5: WHEN the program time is over, THE Oven Controller SHALL sound a signal.
