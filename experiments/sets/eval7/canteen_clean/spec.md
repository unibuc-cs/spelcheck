# Requirements: Canteen

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a student, I want to pay for lunch with my card at the canteen, so that I do not need cash.

### Acceptance Criteria

- C1: WHEN a student taps the card at the till AND the card balance covers the meal, THE Canteen System SHALL take the price from the balance.
- C2: WHEN a student taps the card at the till AND the card balance does not cover the meal, THE Canteen System SHALL refuse the payment.
- C3: WHEN a parent tops up a card, THE Canteen System SHALL add the amount to the card balance.
- C4: WHEN the balance of a card falls below 5 euros, THE Canteen System SHALL send a reminder to the parent.
- C5: WHEN a student leaves the school, THE Canteen System SHALL close the card account and keep the payment history for one year.
