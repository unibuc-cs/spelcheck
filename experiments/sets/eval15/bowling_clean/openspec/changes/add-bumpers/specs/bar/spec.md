## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bumpers

The system SHALL help children to play.

#### Scenario: Bumper lane free

- **WHEN** a player asks for bumpers
- **AND** a lane with bumpers is free
- **THEN** the system raises the bumpers of the lane

#### Scenario: No bumper lane free

- **WHEN** a player asks for bumpers
- **AND** no lane with bumpers is free
- **THEN** the system offers the player a ramp
