# Requirements: Kindergarten

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a parent, I want the kindergarten to check who picks up my child, so that my child goes home only with people I trust.

### Acceptance Criteria

- K1: WHEN an adult comes to pick up a child AND the adult is on the child's pick-up list, THE Kindergarten System SHALL let the child go and record the time.
- K2: WHEN an adult comes to pick up a child AND the adult is not on the child's pick-up list, THE Kindergarten System SHALL keep the child inside and call the parents.
- K3: WHEN a child is absent without notice, THE Kindergarten System SHALL send a text message to the parents.
- K4: WHEN a parent arrives after closing time for the third time in a month, THE Kindergarten System SHALL charge a late fee.
- K5: WHEN a child has a fever, THE Kindergarten System SHALL call the parents and move the child to the quiet room.
- K6: WHILE the parents have turned off notifications, THE Kindergarten System SHALL NOT send text messages to the parents.
