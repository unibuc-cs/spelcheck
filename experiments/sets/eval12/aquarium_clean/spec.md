# Requirements: Aquarium

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As the keeper of a public aquarium, I want the tank controller to look after water and feeding, so that the fish stay healthy.

### Acceptance Criteria

- A1: WHEN the feeding time comes AND the feeder is not empty, THE Tank Controller SHALL release a portion of food into the tank.
- A2: WHEN the feeding time comes AND the feeder is empty, THE Tank Controller SHALL alert the keeper.
- A3: WHEN the water temperature falls below 24 degrees, THE Tank Controller SHALL switch the heater on.
- A4: WHEN the filter clogs for the third time in a week, THE Tank Controller SHALL ask for a new filter cartridge.
- A5: WHEN the water level is more than 5 centimetres below the mark, THE Tank Controller SHALL open the refill valve.
