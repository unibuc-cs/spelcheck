# Requirements: Helpdesk

## Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a customer, I want my support tickets handled quickly, so that my problems get solved.

### Acceptance Criteria

- H1: WHEN a customer submits a ticket, THE Helpdesk System SHALL assign the ticket to an available agent.
- H2: WHEN an agent closes a ticket, THE Helpdesk System SHALL mark the ticket as closed and notify the customer.
- H3: WHEN a customer replies to a closed ticket, THE Helpdesk System SHALL reopen the ticket.
- H4: WHEN a ticket has had no agent reply within 48 hours, THE Helpdesk System SHALL escalate the ticket to a supervisor.
- H5: WHEN a ticket is escalated, THE Helpdesk System SHALL notify the supervisor.
